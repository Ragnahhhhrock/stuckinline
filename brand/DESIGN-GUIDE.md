# Stuck in Line: Design Guide

Source of truth for values: `brand/tokens.json`. This guide explains how to use them.
Everything here is binding under [BRAND-RULE.md](BRAND-RULE.md).

## The idea in one line

A quiet street at night, one long line of ordinary people, and a warm glow at the front that nobody has ever seen inside. Every asset should feel like standing in that line: dark, calm, a little funny, a little uneasy, and curious.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| night | `#0b0d14` | Page and canvas background, darkest tone |
| dusk | `#141623` | Horizon, sky gradient top-to-bottom partner of night |
| ground | `#1a1d2b` | Street surface, panels |
| ink | `#e9e6dc` | Primary text on dark |
| quiet | `#8c93a6` | Secondary text, labels, captions |
| gold | `#ffcf5c` | **You.** The only accent: your marker, your place, primary buttons, the URL |
| goldInk | `#14110a` | Text on gold |
| paper | `#f4f1e8` | Speech bubbles, whites of eyes |
| velvet | `#6a1428` | Stage curtains |
| brass | `#c9a24a` | Stage trim, walking sticks |
| glow | `#fff6d8` | The light at the front |
| kerb | `#4a4e63` | Street edges |
| facade | `#161926` | Building silhouettes |
| windowWarm | `#d9a559` | Lit windows |
| void | `#050507` | The black cube behind the curtains (in-game only) |

Rules of use:

1. **Gold means you.** It marks the player, the player's place and the one action to take. Never use it as decoration, never for more than one idea per layout.
2. **Dark first.** There is no light theme. Backgrounds are night, dusk or ground. White is never a background.
3. **Warm light only comes from the front.** Glow, velvet and brass belong to the stage. Do not spread them across layouts as general colour.
4. Text on night/dusk/ground is ink or quiet. Text on gold is goldInk. Minimum contrast 4.5:1 (all token pairings above meet it).
5. No gradients except sky/ground depth, the stage glow and the light spilling down the street. No rainbow, no neon, no drop-shadow glows on text.

## Type

- Family: the system stack `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`. Raster assets are baked in Inter, its closest open match.
- Headlines: 800 weight, tight leading (about 1.05), sentence case, tabular numerals for places. Question marks are part of the voice; keep them.
- Body: 500 to 600 weight, 16px base, 1.4 line height.
- Labels and the wordmark line: 700, uppercase, wide tracking (about 0.2em), `quiet` colour, small.
- Never stretch, outline, shadow-glow or italicise headlines. Italic is reserved for whispers (the quotes left by people who reached the front).

## Shape and the world

- Everything is **boxy**: rectangles with soft corners, spheres only for eyes and heads on pets. People are a head box, a torso box, two arm boxes, two leg boxes.
- UI uses **pills**: fully rounded buttons and chips, 44px minimum touch height.
- Characters: grown-ups, children, senior citizens (some with a stick), people in wheelchairs, people with walking frames, people on crutches, dogs and cats. Mobility aids are plain boxy frames in `kerb` and `quiet` with dark wheels and tips. They are never gold unless they belong to you, never a joke, and never the whole character. Everyone queues in single file, seen in true perspective toward the stage. Faces are simple: two eyes, brows, a small flat mouth. The expression is patient with a flicker of suspicion.
- **You** are always the one who is marked: gold cone above the head, gold ring at the feet, a gold place pill (`#412` in game, `#?` in teasers).
- Perspective: one vanishing point at the stage. Figures shrink with distance, street kerbs converge on the stage.
- The stage: parted velvet curtains, a brass bar, a bright opening. Nobody is ever shown inside it.
- The black cube (amended 6 October 2026, in-game 3D scene only): when the curtains open, one plain black cube (`void`) can be glimpsed in the opening, far back, partly hidden, only while the curtains are parted. It is lit only by the glow from the front, has no marks, faces, light or motion of its own, and is never shown close up. The rear of the curtains and the space behind them are never shown, and the camera can never go behind the curtains.

## Layout

- Portrait first (390 x 844 design target), then everything else is that layout centred.
- Safe areas respected; one primary action per screen.
- Social and share assets: the question as the headline, the scene beneath or beside it, the URL as a gold pill, the wordmark small and quiet.
- Margins at least 5 to 6 percent of the shorter side. Never place text over faces or the stage.

## Imagery

- Only original, in-world art generated by `scripts/build-brand-assets.py`. No stock photos, no real people, no third-party characters, no logos of other brands.
- Never show the inside of the stage, a winner, a prize, or a real queue. The only exception is the in-game black cube glimpse above; it never appears in raster, social or marketing assets.

### The paid skip product image (amended 6 October 2026)

Square, for the Stripe product page. Same street, same single-file queue, same stage. Differences:

- **You** are still the gold-marked figure, but the place pill reads `#1`. This is the only layout where the pill is not `#?` or the game's real number.
- A plain boxy paper coffee cup stands in the empty foreground: paper body, `kerb` sleeve, `quiet` steam bars, a soft shadow. No brand marks, no brass, no gold, no hearts or faces.
- Headline: "Straight to the front?" in ink, the question as the headline. No price, no button, no exclamation. The URL pill and the quiet wordmark stay as on other assets.
- The stage still shows light only, never the inside, never a person in it.
