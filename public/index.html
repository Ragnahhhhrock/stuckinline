The rules of the line

  1. Joining puts you at the back.
  2. One person goes through the curtains each minute.
  3. Nobody knows what's at the front.
  4. Close the tab and you lose your place.
  5. Reloads and short drops are forgiven. Hiding the tab starts the same clock.
  6. One tab holds your place. Open another and it takes over.
  7. Reach the front and you may leave one line for whoever is behind you.
  8. Then join the back of the line.
